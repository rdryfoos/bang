# CASE: Who Has My Stuff

**First Light house case — business design up front**
**Fill this in before the session. Plain words. No technology.**

The domain is fixed so you do not spend the run-through explaining it. The answers
are yours. Write them the way you would say them out loud to a friend.

## The problem

I lend things and I lose track of them. Later I cannot remember who has what, or
whether it came back.

## Who has it

Me, and anybody who owns things they lend to people they like too much to chase.

## The three things I have lent and want back

Real items. Specific ones. At least one you would be genuinely annoyed to lose.

## The promises

These are what the thing must do to be worth having. Say them as promises, not
features.

1. I can write down an item, who took it, and the day it went out.
2. I can see everything that is still out, without hunting.
3. I can mark something returned, and it stops nagging me.
4. The names of my friends stay on this machine and go nowhere else.

Anything you would add? One or two at most:

## How I would know it worked

The test you would run yourself, in the world, not in the code.

- I lend something on a Tuesday, and three weeks later I can still say who has it
  without opening a drawer.

## What I am assuming

- I am the only person using this. Nobody else needs an account.
- Fewer than a hundred items, ever.
- I will use it on one machine.
